# Privacy Policy – Alembic

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

This policy explains what data is processed when you play **Alembic**, a mobile game published by **DarkNox Studio** ("we", "us").

## 1. Data we do not collect

Alembic does not require an account and never asks for your name, email address or phone number. We do not operate any game server: we do not receive or store any personal data about you.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (in-game currency, stations, upgrades, settings, purchases and your consent choice) is stored **only on your device**. It is deleted if you uninstall the game. Resetting the game from the settings erases your progress but keeps your purchases.

## 3. Advertising

The game offers rewarded ads that are **always optional**: you choose to watch them to get an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by **Unity LevelPlay** and **Unity Ads** (Unity Technologies).

To serve and measure these ads, these services may automatically collect information about your device, including:

- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device model, operating system version, language and mobile carrier;
- ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

**Consent.** On first launch, the game asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, ads may still be shown but they will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

Unity privacy policy: <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>

## 4. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as "Remove ads") are processed by **Google Play**. We never receive your payment information; we only receive confirmation that a product was purchased so that it can be activated in the game.

Google privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 5. Children

Alembic is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold any data about you, requests regarding advertising data should be sent to Unity (link above). You can also withdraw your ad consent in the game at any time. For any question, contact us at **studio.darknox@gmail.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
